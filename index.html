Aquarius Lunar Rover Thermal System

Developed a hybrid passive and active thermal-control architecture for a lunar South Pole rover through NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy.

Selected MLI, Z93P and silver FEP surface treatments, pyrolytic graphite heat spreaders, resistance heaters, and redundant temperature sensing.

Developed requirements to maintain critical battery, avionics, and payload zones between 0°C and 40°C while limiting thermal-control power to 25 W average and 75 W peak.

Technologies: Thermal Systems, Systems Engineering, Trade Studies, Requirements Development, Verification Planning
