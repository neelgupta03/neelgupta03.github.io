ENGINEERING INTERNSHIP

CT Dyno System Modernization

NOV

Project Overview

During my engineering internship at NOV, I worked on the modernization of a coiled tubing dynamometer test system.

The project required understanding how the existing system operated, documenting its mechanical and instrumentation architecture, identifying opportunities for modernization, and supporting system testing and engineering analysis.

Inside the Project

Photos from the dyno system, engineering work, instrumentation, and testing will be shown here to provide a visual look at the system and the work completed during the internship.

Engineering Work

I developed and updated engineering documentation used to better define the existing dyno system and support future modernization.

This included reviewing and documenting instrumentation, P&IDs, I/O architecture, system layouts, controls concepts, system gaps, and potential engineering improvements.

Testing & Validation

I participated in operating the dyno test stand by running fluid through the system at different BPM rates and recording system behavior under changing operating conditions.

The resulting data was analyzed to better understand system performance and evaluate trends observed during testing.

System-Level Engineering

The project required working across mechanical, instrumentation, controls, and operational aspects of the test system rather than focusing on a single isolated component.

This provided hands-on experience understanding how real engineering systems are documented, tested, analyzed, and prepared for modernization.
